Services

Mobile app development

We design, build, and ship your app to the App Store and Play Store — then keep it working as iOS and Android change underneath it. Sign-in, payments, notifications, and the backend that feeds the app are part of the same job, not a separate project.

  • iOS and Android
  • App Store and Play Store release
  • Backend, sign-in, and payments
  • Ongoing maintenance

AI-enabled mobile apps

The AI features worth building are the specific ones: an assistant that answers from your own content, search that understands a question, transcription and summaries that save real time. We build these into the app on top of existing models rather than training our own, which keeps cost and timelines predictable — and we will tell you when a feature does not need AI at all.

  • In-app assistants and chat
  • Search over your own content
  • Transcription and summaries
  • Recommendations

Custom software & websites

Websites, web applications, and the internal tools a business runs on — including the marketing site and admin panel that usually sit behind an app. Built to be handed over, with the code documented for whoever picks it up next.

  • Websites and landing pages
  • Web applications
  • APIs and backends
  • Admin and internal tools

Mobile app security research

Independent review of Android applications, followed by coordinated disclosure to the vendor. This line is self-directed rather than client-commissioned, though we take a small number of paid reviews for teams that own the app.

  • Android apps on Google Play
  • Reported privately to the owner
  • Never published unfixed
  • Paid reviews on request

Who we work with

Most of our work is for people who need an app built — not for companies that already have an engineering team.

  • Founders and early-stage teams

    Getting a first version in front of real users without over-building it. Part of our job is telling you what you can cut from version one.

  • Creators and influencers

    Apps for an audience you already have — membership, community, content, and subscriptions, published on both stores under your own name.

  • Local and small businesses

    Booking, ordering, and customer apps for a business that already works in person and needs to work on a phone.

How we work

We are a small team, and you deal directly with the people doing the building — there is no account manager in between. Engagements are small-batch by design: we take on work where we can stay accountable from the first sketch to the day it goes live.

  • You own everything

    The code, the accounts, and the store listings are yours. When the work is finished you can take it to any developer.

  • Plain language, always

    You will know what is being built and why, without needing a technical background to follow it. Ask for the detail and you will get that too.

  • It has to keep working

    Apps break when iOS and Android change underneath them. We build with that in mind, and we are clear up front about what upkeep involves.
