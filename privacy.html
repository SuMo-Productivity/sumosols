Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 April 2026

This policy describes what data Sumo Solutions ("we", "our", or "us") collects through the sumosols.com website and through email correspondence, and how we handle that data.

1. What we collect

The website is a static, content-only site. We do not run analytics platforms, advertising trackers, or third-party fingerprinting. The data we receive falls into two categories:

  • Hosting-provider logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub maintains web-server logs for traffic to this site (including IP address, browser user-agent, and the URL requested) according to GitHub's own privacy policy. We do not have direct access to these logs and do not retain copies of them ourselves.
  • Email content. Anything you choose to send us by email — your address, your name if you provide it, and the contents of your message.

2. How we use it

We use the data above only to:

  • Reply to your inquiry.
  • Maintain the security of our infrastructure.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Sharing

We share data only where required by law, where needed to investigate misuse of our services, or with infrastructure providers (only the minimum necessary). Such providers are bound by confidentiality.

4. Security

We follow industry-standard practices: HTTPS for all transport, minimal data collection by default, and access controls on any data we hold. No system is perfectly secure, but protecting your data is treated as a core engineering responsibility, not an afterthought.

5. Third-party content

This site loads CSS, fonts, and JavaScript from public CDNs (jsDelivr, cdnjs, Google Fonts). When your browser fetches those resources, the providers may receive standard HTTP request information. Their privacy policies apply to that interaction; please review them on the providers' own websites.

6. Your rights

If you have sent us email, you may ask us to delete that correspondence. We will comply unless we are required to retain it for legal or contractual reasons. Email contact@sumosols.com with any such request.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be highlighted on the homepage for at least one week.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@sumosols.com.
